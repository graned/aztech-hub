## Deliverables
- src/lib/api/http.ts — response helpers (ok, apiError, validationError, notFound, conflict, upstream, mapServiceError, badParam) returning typed NextResponse JSON
- src/lib/api/validation.ts — zod schemas for workspaceId (`^ws-[a-f0-9]{12}$`), sessionId, owner/repo/branch/query; parseBody/parseSearchParams with structured 400 + issues
